A full Android system

LineageOS 20 / Android 13 on an emulated ARM64 CPU, with the Trebuchet launcher, Settings and software graphics. Click the display to interact. Apps use software graphics and may respond slowly. Stopping discards your session.

Startup takes time

Android initializes its system services and apps before showing the launcher. Startup time depends on your device. A black Android display during startup is expected. Keep this tab visible. The VM reserves 2 GiB of RAM and uses your device's CPU.

Browser requirements

Use a current Chromium browser with WebAssembly JSPI. The first visit may reload once to enable browser isolation. This initial demo runs offline.
