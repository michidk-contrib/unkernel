What runs today

Android 9 Bionic, mksh and Toybox on an emulated ARM64 CPU. The terminal connects directly to the guest shell. Closing or stopping the VM discards its changes.

What comes next

The Android launcher, apps and graphical framework are not running yet. Binder IPC is missing from the current kernel. This example is a userspace compatibility milestone.

Browser requirements

Use a current Chromium browser with WebAssembly JSPI. The first visit may reload once to enable browser isolation. No server runs your VM.
