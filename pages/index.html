A full Android system

LineageOS 20 / Android 13 on an emulated ARM64 CPU, with the Trebuchet launcher, Settings and software graphics. Click the display to interact. Stopping discards your session.

Give it a few minutes

Cold boot loads compressed system disks as needed and starts the Android framework. Keep this tab visible. The VM reserves 2 GiB of RAM and uses your device's CPU.

Browser requirements

Use a current Chromium browser with WebAssembly JSPI. The first visit may reload once to enable browser isolation. This initial demo runs offline.
